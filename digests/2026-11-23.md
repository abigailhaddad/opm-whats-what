## 2. Mandatory eFile now required for FLSA and other administrative claims

FLSA claims and certain administrative claims must be filed through eFile as of September 29, 2026, unless OPM grants a discretionary exemption. OPM will not find good cause for a late settlement-claim filing if no exemption was requested before the deadline, and it declined to toll deadlines while exemption requests are pending, though requests may be made by phone or email.

*[FLSA Claims and Compliance](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23730/flsa-claims-and-compliance) (Rule, 2026-11-20) · [Procedures for Settling Claims](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/20/2026-23731/procedures-for-settling-claims) (Rule, 2026-11-20)*

- **Mandatory eFile with discretionary exemption; late-filing risk if no exemption requested**. OPM may exempt a party from eFiling in its sole and exclusive discretion, and will not find good cause for a late filing if the party failed to ask for an exemption before the deadline. All five commenters objected. OPM kept the provision without fixed criteria, response deadline, or reconsideration process, but said it applies a 'cannot reasonably file electronically' standard. (p. 75164)
- **OPM declines to toll deadlines during pending eFile exemption requests**. OPM rejected Comment 03's proposed text that would automatically toll deadlines while an exemption request is pending, or treat a non-electronic filing as timely once an exemption issues. A claimant's back-pay recovery date still depends on when OPM or the agency receives the claim, so delay in obtaining an exemption can affect recoverable back pay. OPM instead promises case-by-case consideration of timely good-faith contact. (p. 75167)
- **OPM adds phone/email exemption requests and good-cause factors, but only as preamble statements**. Exemption requests may be made by the telephone number or email in the FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT caption, not only through OPM's website, and are treated the same as website requests. OPM will also treat eFile outages and claimant literacy, disability, or other barriers as bearing on good cause. These are statements of practice; no regulatory text changed. (p. 75165)
