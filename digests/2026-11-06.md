## 1. 2027 GS pay frozen; law enforcement special rates rise 3.8% to 6.7%

GS base and locality pay stay at 2026 levels in 2027, while about 140,000 employees on law enforcement special rate tables L001–L140 get 3.8% effective January 10, 2027. Newly covered law enforcement categories get about 6.7% automatically unless their agency opts out by November 25, 2026.

*[Request for Information: Special Rates to Achieve January 2027 Pay Adjustments for Certain Law Enforcement Personnel](https://www.opm.gov/chcoc/latest-memos/request-for-information-special-rates-to-achieve-january-2027-pay-adjustments-for-certain-law-enforcement-personnel.pdf) (CHCO memo, 2026-11-06)*

- **GS base pay and locality pay frozen at 2026 levels for 2027**. Under the President's alternative plan, GS and other statutory base pay rates and locality percentages stay at 2026 levels in January 2027. Only targeted special-rate increases are provided. (p. 1)
- **3.8% raise for about 140,000 employees on Special Rate Tables L001–L140, effective January 10, 2027**. All existing law enforcement special rate tables L001–L140 rise 3.8%. They currently cover about 140,000 employees. The increase takes effect the first pay period on or after January 1, 2027 (January 10, 2027). (p. 1)
- **Expanded coverage is automatic unless the agency opts out by November 25, 2026**. New special rate coverage applies by default. To opt out, an agency must send a letter from the agency head or a designee (e.g., CHCO or HR Director) to PayPolicy@opm.gov by November 25, 2026. The same deadline applies to opt-outs for non-GS positions and to questions or comments. (p. 2)

## 2. OPM finds 50,000 deceased people still enrolled in FEHB

A match against Treasury death data found about 50,000 deceased individuals still listed in FEHB, with $96.3 million paid after death ($70.6 million in government premium contributions and $25.7 million in retirement payments) plus an estimated $10.4 million in carrier costs. OPM will next review medical claims paid after date of death, a cost category not included in these totals.

*[OPM Review Identifies $106.7 Million in Improper Payments in FEHBP](https://www.opm.gov/news/news-releases/opm-review-identifies-dollar1067-million-in-improper-payments-in-fehbp/) (News release, 2026-11-02)*

- **About 50,000 deceased individuals found still enrolled in FEHB**. OPM matched Treasury's August 2026 DRCST death data against FEHB enrollment records and found about 50,000 deceased people still listed in the program. (p. 1)
- **$96.3M paid after death: $70.6M in premium contributions, $25.7M in retirement payments**. The review found $96.3 million in improper post-death payments. This includes $70.6 million in government FEHB premium contributions and $25.7 million in retirement (annuity) payments. (p. 1)
- **OPM will next review medical claims paid after date of death**. OPM plans a further review in the coming weeks of claims that may have been paid after enrollees died. This is a cost category not included in the $106.7 million total. (p. 1)
